# Objectives

## Main Objective

To develop a secure, transparent, and user-friendly NGO Donation & Impact Tracking Dashboard.

## Specific Objectives

- Simplify the online donation process.
- Improve transparency in fund utilization.
- Allow NGOs to manage campaigns efficiently.
- Enable donors to track their donations.
- Generate reports for administrators.
- Provide secure user authentication.
- Maintain all donation records in a centralized database.
