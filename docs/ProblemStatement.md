# Problem Statement

## NGO Donation & Impact Tracking Dashboard

Many NGOs face challenges in collecting donations and maintaining transparency with donors. Donors often do not know how their contributions are utilized, which reduces trust and willingness to donate.

Most existing systems do not provide a centralized platform where NGOs can manage campaigns, donors can donate securely, and administrators can monitor activities.

This project aims to develop a web-based NGO Donation & Impact Tracking Dashboard that provides a secure, transparent, and user-friendly platform for NGOs, donors, and administrators. The system enables online donations, campaign management, donation tracking, and impact reporting to improve transparency and build donor confidence.# Problem Statement
